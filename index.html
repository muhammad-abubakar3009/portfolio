Behind the build

I graduated with a BS in Computer Science from the University of the Punjab (PUCIT), with a strong foundation in object-oriented programming, data structures & algorithms, and database design. Before that, I spent over a year freelancing — building and customizing eCommerce stores on WordPress and Shopify, integrating payment gateways, and managing SEO for a US-based client.

That freelance work taught me how real systems get used: inventory that has to stay accurate, checkouts that can't break, data that has to be trustworthy. I've since applied that same practical instinct to full-stack development — most recently building Salon OS, a full-stack salon management platform with JWT authentication, role-based access control, and transactional data integrity, built solo from database schema to UI.

My final year project, a Smart Resume Screening System, pulled together everything from that degree — data modeling, recommendation logic, and a real user-facing application.
